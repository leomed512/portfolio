GIS, Data, and Environmental Analysis

Professional experience applying geospatial analysis, automation, and data processing to environmental and operational challenges.

Environmental Consultant

Operational Response Unit (URO) · Milla River Early Warning System

Apr 2026–Present

Contribute to the modernization of the Milla River Early Warning System, encompassing sensor deployment, infrastructure improvements, and environmental assessment. My work has focused on developing the geospatial and hydrometeorological components by collecting and processing data for watershed characterization and producing maps and indicators to support disaster risk management. Using Python, I automated the cleaning and consolidation of more than 115,000 meteorological records from two weather stations and integrated these time series with territorial variables for the watershed’s hydrometeorological analysis.

Data Analyst

Transportation-Sector Client (Spain)

Jun–Dec 2025

Built Python- and SQL-based ETL pipelines to consolidate operational data from Excel, CSV, and PostgreSQL sources. Conducted geospatial analyses with GeoPandas to identify passenger-flow and incident patterns by station and route. The resulting datasets supported Power BI dashboards used to detect location-specific operational performance issues.

Data Analyst and Automation Specialist

Odontology Coach (Spain)

Feb 2025–Jan 2026

Designed and automated performance indicators to monitor dental clinic operations, implementing data-entry and validation workflows in Google Sheets using JavaScript and Google Apps Script. The automated reports replaced manual processes for tracking patients, treatments, and marketing campaigns.

Field Engineer and Botanical Consultant

Mérida Botanical Garden · University of Los Andes

2019 · 2021–2022

Using an inventory of more than 200 plant species compiled through multiple field surveys at the botanical garden, I developed geospatial databases and thematic maps in QGIS to visualize species distribution patterns across different garden zones. The results contributed to three scientific publications.
