Selected Experience

Forestry & Environmental Work

Environmental Consultant

Operational Response Unit (URO) · Milla River Early Warning System

Apr 2026–Present
(Remote)

Contribute to a proposal to modernize the Milla River community-based early warning system, encompassing sensor deployment, infrastructure improvements, and environmental assessment. My work has focused primarily on the environmental component, including watershed characterization—geomorphology, hydrology, and land cover—the integration of geospatial data into project mapping, automated meteorological data acquisition, and the preparation of technical reports.

Forestry Engineer

Mérida Botanical Garden · University of Los Andes

2019 · 2021–2022

Conducted an inventory and documented more than 200 tree, shrub, and herbaceous plant species at the botanical garden through georeferencing, specimen collection, taxonomic identification, and photographic documentation. This fieldwork, combined with a review of ecological and ethnobotanical literature, contributed to three scientific publications.

Botanical Consultant

Fundación Programa Andes Tropicales

Oct–Nov 2021

Conducted field surveys and subsequent data processing to identify more than 60 vascular plant species in support of the conservation of lagoons and wetlands within a national park. Analyzed variations in floristic distribution in relation to elevation and proximity to water, and translated the findings into technical reports and thematic maps used to inform conservation planning.

Agroforestry Research Assistant

Institute of Agricultural Research (IIAP) · University of Los Andes

2015–2019

Monitored a cacao-based agroforestry system incorporating timber species such as Spanish cedar, mahogany, and pink trumpet tree, as well as experimental greenhouse operations, over a four-year period. Responsibilities included plant health assessments, forest mensuration, measurement of physiological variables such as photosynthetic performance and water stress, and comprehensive agronomic management—pruning, fertilization, irrigation, and weed control. Also provided technical assistance to local producers in crop and nursery management.
